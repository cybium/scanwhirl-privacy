ScanWhirl Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 · App: ScanWhirl (package com.scanwhirl.app) · Developer: Cybium

This policy explains how the ScanWhirl Android app ("ScanWhirl", "the app", "we") handles your information. In short: ScanWhirl does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not send your scanned documents to our servers. We do not have servers.

1. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from you. Specifically, we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers, and we do not run our own analytics or advertising.

2. Camera and your scanned documents

  • Camera: ScanWhirl asks for camera permission only to let you scan documents. The camera is used only while you are scanning.
  • Scans and files: Pages you scan, and any PDFs or images you create, are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to us. You decide whether to keep, share, or delete them.
  • Storage: The app may use storage access to save or export your scans on your device. Files only leave your device if you choose to share them (for example, via email or a messaging app).

3. Third-party services

ScanWhirl uses the Google ML Kit Document Scanner, provided through Google Play services, to detect page edges and enhance scans. Document processing is designed to run on your device. Google may download or update the scanner component over the internet and may collect limited technical and usage data (such as performance metrics, crash information, and device details) in line with Google's policies. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • ML Kit data disclosure

We do not control Google's data practices.

4. Permissions used

  • Camera – to scan documents.
  • Internet / network state – so Google Play services can download or update the scanning component.
  • Storage – to save scans on older Android versions.

5. Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone, because we do not have it. The only parties involved are Google (see section 3) and any app or person you choose to send your scans to.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your scans stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Security

Your scans are kept in your device's storage and protected by your device's own security (such as your screen lock). No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

8. Children's privacy

ScanWhirl is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in a new app version.
